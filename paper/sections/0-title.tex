\section*{Team and Responsibilities}

\begin{description}
\item[Kaveen Jayamanna (KJ).] The planner and its cost model
  (\texttt{src/\_plan.py}): the box-swap graph, the shortest-path search, and
  the derivation of $\tau$, $\mu$ and $D_{post}^{*}$. The in-place
  cycle-following CUDA kernel (\texttt{src/\_kernels/}), including the scalar
  and vectorised paths.

\item[Alexis Newman (AN).] Measurement and validation: the throughput
  micro-benchmark that calibrates the cost model on the evaluation hardware,
  the correctness suite that gates every result, the end-to-end comparison
  against the out-of-place baseline, and the head-to-head against the closest
  prior systems.
\end{description}
