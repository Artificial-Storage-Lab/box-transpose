\section{Milestones and Timeline}
\label{sec:milestones}

Table~\ref{tab:timeline} gives the plan, one milestone per week over
\timelineWeeks{} weeks, with every milestone assigned to one of the
\timelineMembers{} members named on the title page. The split follows the
division of responsibilities stated there: the planner and cost-model
milestones are Kaveen's, the measurement and validation milestones are
Alexis's, and the milestones marked to both are genuinely joint work. Those
are the comparison against prior systems and the write-up.

The ordering is not arbitrary: the calibration milestones come first because
every later measurement is interpreted through the cost model, and a number
produced before $\tau$ and $\mu$ are re-derived would have to be produced
again afterwards.

Weeks are numbered from the week this proposal is submitted rather than dated,
so the schedule can be anchored to the course calendar without rewriting it.
