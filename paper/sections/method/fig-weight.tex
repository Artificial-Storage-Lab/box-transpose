\caption{\capheadline{Where an edge's weight comes from.}
Each cell carries its flat position. Shaded cells are the ones a transpose maps
to themselves --- they are already in the right place. We skip them; a copy has
to write them anyway. The smaller the block, the larger the share that stays
put, so a $\modelMinMovedRows \times \modelMinMovedCols$ block is the best any
single swap can do.}
